# Exploring Joara

Exploring Joara is a public archive of site photographs and digital representations of archaeological artifacts. Joara archaeologists maintain its content through a private staff interface.

## Language

**Archive**:
The publicly browsable collection of Joara pictures and artifacts.
_Avoid_: Staff dashboard, storage bucket

**Picture**:
A photograph from the Joara site, presented without required descriptive catalog fields. Each uploaded picture belongs to one picture folder; separately uploaded copies are independent.
_Avoid_: Artifact, scan

**Picture folder**:
A named grouping of pictures that may contain further picture folders. Staff and visitors use the same hierarchy.
_Avoid_: Album (implies a flat grouping), collection (ambiguous with artifacts)

**Artifact**:
An archive entry representing an archaeological object through an interactive 3D scan, a name, a description, and an uploaded thumbnail.
_Avoid_: Picture, photograph

**Artifact thumbnail**:
A separately uploaded image representing an artifact before a visitor opens its interactive scan. It is required before the artifact is publicly visible.
_Avoid_: Automatically generated preview

**Editor**:
A signed-in staff member who maintains archive content.
_Avoid_: Visitor, publisher (there is no separate publishing role in the first release)

**Visitor**:
A person browsing the public archive without needing an account.

**Timeline**:
A public page displaying an externally hosted timeline within an embedded frame.

**Virtual museum**:
A public section reserved for a future museum experience, represented by an editable placeholder in the first release.

**Site section**:
One of Pictures, Artifacts, Timeline, or Virtual Museum, reached through the site's navigation.
_Avoid_: Destination

**Home card**:
An image, description, and link introducing a site section on the homepage.
_Avoid_: Destination card
